Two ways to count, and both are shown

        
Ask how many days until an exam on Friday and nobody means 3 days,
        14 hours and 22 minutes. They mean three days. The big
        number counts whole calendar days, and it changes at midnight, which is
        how people actually talk.

        
The live line underneath does the other thing: it counts down to the
        exact instant you set, and it ticks over at that time rather than at
        midnight. Both are correct, they answer different questions, and every
        argument about a countdown being "wrong" is really about which of the
        two someone meant.


        
Today counts as zero

        
A date set to today shows nought days, and tomorrow shows one. That
        is the everyday reading of the words. A leave application or a notice
        period usually wants both the first and last day counted instead, which
        is a different sum — the date difference tool has a tick box for
        exactly that.


        
Working days

        
The working-day figure counts Monday to Friday between now and the
        target. As everywhere on this site, it takes no account of
        public holidays, because they differ by state, by year and by
        employer. Subtract your own.


        
Frequently asked questions

        

          
Does the count include today?

          
No. The big number is how many whole days are left, so a date
          tomorrow shows one and a date today shows zero. That matches the way
          people say it out loud. If you need both days counted, as a leave
          application would, use the date difference tool and tick the box
          there.

        

        

          
Why does the day count change at midnight rather than at the time I set?

          
Because the big number counts calendar days, which is what people
          mean by how many days until something. The live line below it counts
          down to the exact moment instead, so it ticks over at the time you
          set. The two are deliberately different and both are shown.

        

        

          
Can it count days since a date that has passed?

          
Yes. Pick any past date and it counts up instead of down, saying
          how long ago it was. It is the same arithmetic with the sign the
          other way round, so anniversaries and how long since I started work
          both work.

        

        

          
Is anything I type sent anywhere?

          
No. The arithmetic happens in JavaScript inside your own browser.
          Nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved - close the tab and it is
          gone.
