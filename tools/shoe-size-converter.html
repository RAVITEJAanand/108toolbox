India uses UK sizes

This is the useful shortcut: an Indian size is a UK size. Shoes sold in India are marked on the British system, so an Indian 8 and a UK 8 are the same shoe. From there, US men's sizes run about one number higher and US women's about two, while EU sizes are on a different scale entirely.

That one-number gap is why ordering from an American site so often goes wrong. A man who wears 8 in India is usually a US 9. Ordering "8" because that is the number he knows brings a shoe a full size too small.

The number that actually means something

A shoe size is not a measurement of your foot. It describes the last — the mould the shoe was built around — and every brand uses its own lasts. Two shoes both marked UK 8 can differ by most of a size, which is why a brand you trust fits and a new one does not.

Foot length in centimetres is the only figure that means the same thing everywhere, and it is shown here for that reason. To measure: stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall, mark the tip of your longest toe, and measure from wall to mark. Do it in the evening, when feet have swollen slightly, and measure both — most people have one foot a little larger, and that is the one to fit. Many brands publish a centimetre chart, and when they do, it beats the size number every time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Indian shoe size the same as the UK size?

Yes. India uses the British sizing system, so an Indian 8 is a UK 8. That is why an Indian size converts cleanly to UK and needs a step up of roughly one for US men and two for US women.

How do I measure my foot length at home?

Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall, mark the tip of your longest toe, and measure from the wall to the mark. Do it in the evening, when feet are at their largest, and measure both feet — most people have one slightly bigger, and that is the one to fit.

Why does the same size fit differently in different brands?

Because a size describes the last the shoe was built on, not your foot, and every brand uses its own lasts. Two shoes marked UK 8 can differ by most of a size. Foot length in centimetres is the only figure that means the same thing everywhere, which is why it is shown here.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The conversion happens in JavaScript inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved — close the tab and it is gone.
