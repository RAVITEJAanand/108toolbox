About 108 ToolBox

108 ToolBox is a growing collection of small, single-purpose web tools. The goal is simple: you arrive with one job, you finish it in under ten seconds, and you leave.

How it works

Every tool is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and runs inside your browser. When you compress an image here, the image is read by your own computer, resized by your own computer, and handed back to you. It is never sent to a server, because there is no server to send it to.

Why 108?

It is the target. 56 tools are live today and the rest are being added in small batches. Each new tool gets its own page so it can be bookmarked and shared on its own.

What this site will never do

  • Ask you to create an account
  • Put a watermark on your output
  • Hold a result back behind a paywall
  • Upload your files anywhere
